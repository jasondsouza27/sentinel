Auto-scan demo

  1. Start Sentinel and load the extension folder unpacked in Chrome.
  2. Open the Sentinel toolbar popup on this page and turn on Auto-scan this site.
  3. Markers appear beside risky messages. Click one to see the evidence.
  4. Press Simulate incoming message: new messages are scanned as they arrive.
  5. Turn on Read text in images too. The pictures in the chat are then read on your computer, including the QR code.

← Back to the Sentinel app

Sentinel reads only visible text (and, if you allow it, images) on sites you turn on. Nothing is saved, and content goes only to the Sentinel app on this computer.
