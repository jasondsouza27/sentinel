04 / ACCOUNTS

Social media bot account detection

Estimate whether an account behaves like a person, an automated bot, or part of a coordinated campaign. Sentinel uses only official APIs or data you provide, and stores nothing.

iThe score is a heuristic bot-likeness index, not a probability. No single signal proves an account is a bot, and automated accounts are not necessarily malicious.
